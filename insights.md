# Key Insights – IPL Dashboard (2008–2024)

1. **Virat Kohli is the all-time leading run scorer** with 8,014 runs, ahead of Shikhar Dhawan (6,769) and Rohit Sharma (6,630), a gap of more than 1,200 runs over second place.
2. **Yuzvendra Chahal leads the wicket-takers** with 205 wickets, followed by Piyush Chawla (192) and Dwayne Bravo (183). Spin bowlers take 2 of the top 3 spots.
3. **Gujarat Titans have the highest win percentage (62.2%)**, followed by Chennai Super Kings (58.0%) and Mumbai Indians (55.2%). Note that Gujarat Titans joined the league only in 2022, so their figure is based on far fewer matches than CSK or MI.
4. **Matches are high-scoring:** the average match has about 318 total runs (both innings combined), and the highest-scoring match reached 549 runs.
5. The dataset covers 1,095 matches across 17 seasons, and the dashboard filters allow drilling into any season or team.
